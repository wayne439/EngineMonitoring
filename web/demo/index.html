Engine monitoring for piston aircraft

Know your engine before it tells you.

Engine Pulse reads the data your avionics already record, checks every flight against your own POH limits, and watches for the slow changes that come before a failure. This demo runs on the real flights of N63KA, a turbonormalized Cirrus SR22.

  • 1Tap the health gauge to see what scored each flight.
  • 2Open FLIGHTS and pick any flight for its full report.
  • 3Open TRENDS to scroll a year of cruise data.
After every flight

Checked against your POH

Limits are read from your own POH and AFM supplements, cross-checked, then locked. Every flight is checked against them, second by second.

Across flights

Early warning

Each cruise is corrected for outside air temperature and fuel flow, then compared with your engine's own normal range. Drifts show up flights before they become limits.

One number

Engine health

A 0-100 score from your last ten flights, green, yellow or red, with a trend arrow and the exact reason for every point lost.

No more SD card juggling

Easy uploads

Drop logs and documents on the website from any computer, or share them from your iPad. Garmin Perspective and G1000 logs work today.
