How it works

Three steps, then it runs after every flight.

Upload your POH and logs

Add your POH PDF and your engine log files, from a computer on this website or straight from your iPad or iPhone. The app reads the Section 2 engine limits from your POH, cross-checks them against any AFM supplement, then locks them.

Every flight is checked

Each flight is checked against those limits: RPM, CHT, oil temperature and pressure, bus voltage, and TIT and manifold pressure on turbo engines. You also get per-cylinder CHT and EGT, a cruise card, the run-up mag check and more.

Early warning across flights

Cruise values are corrected for outside air temperature and fuel flow and compared with your engine's own normal range. Shifts and slow drifts show up flights before they reach a limit.

Early warning

A baseline from your engine's own history, values corrected for OAT and fuel flow, and SHIFT and DRIFT detection. Sparklines on HOME show each value against its normal band.

Limits from your POH

Your POH PDF is read on the device. Section 2 limits come out with their page numbers and are cross-checked against AFM supplements, such as the Tornado Alley turbonormalizer, which govern where they differ. Then the profile locks.

Ask your manuals

ASK searches your POH and any manuals you upload, such as the Garmin pilot's guide, so the answer comes from your own documents.

AD check

FAA airworthiness directives from the Federal Register for your aircraft model, engine and propeller, with links to the FAA's Dynamic Regulatory System (DRS).

Maintenance

Service history, reminders and oil-change tracking, alongside the flights they relate to.

FAQ

Questions pilots ask.

Is Engine Pulse FAA-approved?

No. Engine Pulse is an advisory tool. It doesn't replace your POH/AFM, your engine monitor or your A&P, and the pilot in command is responsible for the aircraft. Use it to notice things earlier and to have better conversations with your mechanic.

Which aircraft and avionics does it work with?

Your aircraft, if its avionics or engine monitor records a data log. Garmin G1000 and G1000 NXi, Cirrus Perspective, Perspective+ and Perspective Touch+, and the JPI EDM-830 are tested with real logs. Other JPI EDM models, Garmin G3X Touch, G3000, TXi and GI 275, EI MVP-50 and CGR-30, Dynon SkyView, Avidyne Entegra R9, and GRT, AFS and MGL exports are in beta. On twins, the left engine is analyzed for now.

Why do you need my POH?

So every check uses your aircraft's real limits, not generic numbers. The app reads the Section 2 limits from your POH PDF on your device, notes the page each came from, and cross-checks them against any AFM supplement you upload. Where a supplement differs, the supplement governs.

Can I add more than one aircraft?

Yes. In the app, use the aircraft menu at the top of the sidebar and choose Add another aircraft: make, model, tail number and avionics. Switch between aircraft from the same menu, or from AIRCRAFT > Your aircraft.

Each aircraft keeps its own flights, POH, maintenance and notes. On the upload page, choose the aircraft at the top before you add files.

Why is my aircraft profile locked?

Once the limits are read and checked, the profile locks so they can't be changed by accident. If something needs to change, such as an engine swap or a new STC, email sales@enginepulse.net and we'll unlock it.

Where is my data stored, and who can see it?

Your account, flights and documents are stored with Supabase in the US (West, Oregon). Row-level security means each account can see only its own data. We don't show ads, track you or sell your data. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Do I need both an iPad and an iPhone?

No. Engine Pulse runs on either, with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. If you use both, sign in with the same account and everything syncs.

What does it cost?

Free during the beta. After the App Store launch it's $14.99 a month after a 15-day free trial, billed through Apple. You can cancel any time in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before renewal.

Can I upload wirelessly from the airplane?

Not directly. You upload from a computer on the upload page or from your iPad or iPhone. With a Garmin GDL 60 or Flight Stream 510, download the flight from your Garmin account or share it from Garmin's app first.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open ACCOUNT and choose Delete account. That deletes your data from the cloud as well. See Support for more.
