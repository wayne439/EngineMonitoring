Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026. This policy covers the Engine Pulse app for iPad and iPhone and this website, both provided by Engine Pulse.

In short

We collect what Engine Pulse needs to analyze your engine and keep your data in sync, and we use it only to run the service. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account and its cloud data from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

We collect only what you give us or what the app needs to work:

Engine data logs can include position, altitude and time. Engine Pulse uses them for engine analysis; we don't build a location history of you or share your flights with anyone.

We don't use advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking tools in the app or on this website, and this website doesn't set tracking cookies. The upload page stores your sign-in session in your browser so you stay signed in.

How we use it

We use your data only to run Engine Pulse for you:

  • to sign you in and keep your account secure;
  • to analyze your flights against your aircraft's limits and its own history;
  • to keep your flights, profile, maintenance and documents in sync across your devices;
  • to send emails you need, such as confirmation codes and password resets; and
  • to answer you when you contact support.

We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to profile you. We won't share it with anyone else unless you ask us to, or unless the law requires it.

When the app checks for airworthiness directives, it sends your aircraft, engine and propeller model to the public Federal Register API. It doesn't send your name, email or tail number.

Sign in with Apple

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple confirms who you are and shares a unique identifier with us, plus your name and email if you choose to share them. If you hide your email, Apple gives us a private relay address that forwards to you. We never see your Apple ID password.

Payments

Engine Pulse is free during the beta. After the App Store launch, subscriptions are billed by Apple. Apple handles your payment details; we receive only whether your subscription is active. This website doesn't take payments.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data until you delete your account. To delete it, open the app, go to ACCOUNT and choose Delete account. This removes your account and its cloud data: your profile, flights, maintenance records and uploaded files.

Copies on your own devices stay there until you delete the app. Our providers may keep encrypted backups for a short time before they are overwritten.

If you can't get into the app, email sales@enginepulse.net from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Files are kept in private storage, and row-level security limits every database read and write to the account that owns it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your devices locked.

Children

Engine Pulse isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices

  • See or correct your data: most of it is visible and editable in the app. Locked aircraft limits can be changed through support.
  • Get a copy: email us and we'll send you a copy of the data we hold for your account.
  • Delete: use Delete account in the app, or email us.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under local law. Contact us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
