Set up Engine Pulse in 10 minutes

Four steps on your iPad or iPhone. Keep this page open and follow along. Questions: sales@enginepulse.net.

Steps 1. Install the app 2. Register 3. Bring in your flights 4. Upload your POH If something goes wrong

1. Install the app

  1. On your iPad or iPhone, install TestFlight from the App Store (it's Apple's free app for beta software).
  2. On the same device, open the Engine Pulse beta link and tap Accept, then Install.

Open the Engine Pulse beta in TestFlight

Reading this on a computer? Point your iPad or iPhone camera at the code. Typing the link instead? It's testflight.apple.com/join/V9XX24Ca with the capitals exactly as shown (V, X, X, C), or it says Not Found.

You don't need an Apple developer account. If a page says "Sign in to accept your invitation" from App Store Connect, close it and use the link above.

2. Register

  1. Open Engine Pulse and tap Register.
  2. Enter your name, email and a password, then your aircraft: make, model, tail number and avionics (the usual avionics fill in; change them if yours differ).
  3. Tap Register. If you're asked for a 6-digit code, it's in your email (check spam or junk).
  4. More than one aircraft? Register with one, then add the others from the aircraft menu at the top of the sidebar (Add another aircraft).

Use one email everywhere. The same email and password work on every iPad and iPhone and on the upload website. Already registered on this website? Tap Log in in the app instead.

3. Bring in your flights

After you register, the app opens Get started (it's also on HOME until your first flight arrives). Pick the way that suits you:

From a computer (easiest)

  1. Put your avionics' SD card or your engine monitor's USB stick in the computer.
  2. Go to enginepulse.net/upload and log in with the same email and password as the app.
  3. More than one aircraft? Choose the aircraft at the top of the page first.
  4. Drag the whole data_log folder (Garmin), the .JPI file (JPI EDM) or your monitor's CSV files onto Engine monitor log files.
  5. Open Engine Pulse and tap Check for my flights. They also come in by themselves whenever the app opens.

Straight into the iPad

  1. Plug the SD card or USB stick into the iPad with a USB-C card reader.
  2. In Get started, tap Choose the card's folder and pick the data_log folder (or the folder on the stick). Every flight is added.
  3. More than one aircraft? Check the Adding to line above the import buttons first, and pick another aircraft there if needed.

Where the files are

  • Garmin G1000 / NXi, Cirrus Perspective, Perspective+ and Touch+: the SD card in the top slot of the MFD, folder data_log. Take it out on the ground and put it back before you fly.
  • Garmin G3X, TXi, GI 275: the SD card in the display, folder data_log.
  • J.P. Instruments EDM: USB stick in the EDM, hold STEP and LF for 5 seconds, tap STEP to DUMP?, choose NEW or ALL, tap STEP. The file is named like U241015.JPI.
  • Electronics International, Dynon, Avidyne R9 and others: export the flight data log to a USB stick as CSV (see your monitor's manual).
  • Garmin GDL 60 or Flight Stream 510: in the app, Get started > My flights are in Garmin's apps shows the Garmin Pilot and flyGarmin steps.

The last 20 or more flights give the best baseline.

4. Upload your POH

Upload your POH / AFM as one PDF, including the Section 9 supplements: on enginepulse.net/upload (POH box) or in the app under AIRCRAFT > Documents and uploads. Engine Pulse reads your engine limits from it and locks your aircraft profile. Uploaded it twice? Swipe left on one in Documents and uploads to delete it.

That's it. HOME now shows your engine's health score, every flight is checked against your limits, and early warning watches the trends.

If something goes wrong

  • The TestFlight link says Not Found: the capitals matter. Tap the button above instead of typing it.
  • "Sign in to accept your invitation" (App Store Connect): that's not for testers. Close it and use the TestFlight link.
  • No code email: check spam or junk, wait a few minutes, then tap Resend in the app.
  • Uploaded, but no flights in the app: check that the app is signed in (ACCOUNT) with the same email you used on the website, then tap Check for my flights in Get started.
  • A file won't import: email it to sales@enginepulse.net with your tail number and we'll add support for it.

Anything else: email us with your tail number and a screenshot. A real person answers, usually the same day.
